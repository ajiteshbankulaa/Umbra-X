# Uncertainty, Sensitivity, and Limitations

This document establishes where Umbra-X results are robust, where outcomes invert across plausible modeling choices, and what physical phenomena are outside scope.

## 1. Quantified Sensitivities (`convergence_benchmark.json`)

- **LOLA Vertical Error (`0σ -> 3σ`):** On rim pads grazing low solar elevation angles, calibrated terrain uncertainty shifts uninterrupted darkness substantially: under the current apparent (LT+S) ephemeris, nominal `0σ` darkness on `shackleton-ridge pad 481` is `52.25 h` (`52.3 h` rounded in UI, crossing the `48 h` limit; in the refreshed fixed-disk benchmark `convergence_benchmark.json`, Pad #481’s full-catalog low-light stretch changes by about 21 h under `0σ–3σ` height perturbations), while `malapert-massif pad 481` flips from `0.00 h` (continuous Sun) to multi-hour darkness at `3σ`. Results evaluated at `0σ` must therefore be treated as **Sensitive**.
- **Regional Terrain Extent (`48 km` vs `96 km` vs `144 km`):** Elevated rim peaks (`shackleton pad 481`) converge by `96 km` (`<= 0.008°` beyond `96 km`), whereas lower ridge/flank pads (`shackleton pad 181`) encounter additional horizon occlusion between `96 km` and `144 km` (`+3.560°` peak ridge, expanding longest Earth blackout from `238.67 h` to `323.83 h`). Any pad whose `48 km` vs `96 km` outage differs or flips threshold is explicitly flagged **Sensitive** in the UI.
- **Spatial DEM Resolution (`80 m` live vs `20 m + 80 m` hybrid):** Using pure `80 m/px` LOLA (`live_terrain.py` fallback) smooths acute near-field rim peaks by up to `1.231°` (`malapert pad 481`) and `0.602°` (`shackleton pad 481`), causing `shackleton pad 481` at `5 m` mast to falsely report `0.0 h` darkness (`52.25 h` in `20 m + 80 m` hybrid). Live `80 m` queries are labeled **Sensitive** for this reason.
- **Slope Definition & Sub-20 m Hazards:** Per-site `slopeBenchmark` comparisons across all `961` pads (`planner.json`) measure `median` / `p95` absolute differences vs. official PGDA `LDSM_80S_20MPP_ADJ.TIF` of `0.1753°` / `0.7066°` at Shackleton Ridge (`pad 481`: `5.6005°` compiled vs `5.6592°` LDSM), `0.0794°` / `0.3111°` at Mons Mouton (`2.4572°` vs `2.4361°`), and `0.2196°` / `0.8144°` at Malapert Massif (`5.2885°` fail vs `4.8527°` pass right across the `5°` threshold!). Furthermore, `20 m/px` terrain **cannot detect meter-scale boulders, small impact craters, or geotechnical bearing capacity** required for lander leg stability.
- **Boundary Coverage (`-80°S`):** Live queries north of `~-81°S` hit the `LDEM_80S_80MPP_ADJ.TIF` tile edge (`< 360` covered azimuths), making outage maxima strictly optimistic lower bounds and forcing an **Incomplete** classification.
- **Solar & Temporal Discretization:** Between `5-min` and `1-min` DE440 sampling, total solar hours vary `<= 0.083 h`. Between `1.0°` and `0.25°` azimuth bins, horizon interpolation differs by `<= 0.101°` (`<= 0.083 h` dark shift). Finite 2D solar disk vs 1D straight-limb chord differs by `<= 0.028` fractional exposure (`-0.667 h` at `shackleton pad 481`). Mast height remains a first-order lever (`1.5 m` vs `5.0 m` reduces longest darkness from `279.3 h` to `52.25 h` at `shackleton pad 481`).

## 2. Explicit Epistemic Boundaries (`Not modeled`)

Umbra-X **does not model** and must never be cited as establishing:
1. **Radio-Frequency (RF) Link Feasibility or DSN Scheduling:** Geometric Earth/station line-of-sight above `10°` elevation omits carrier-to-noise link budgets, antenna gain, local terrain multipath/diffraction, and multi-mission DSN scheduling conflicts.
2. **Ground-Truth Surface Ice / Volatile Abundance:** `psr_mask.bin` (`LPSR_80S_20MPP_ADJ.TIF`) identifies geomorphically persistently shadowed zones only; water-ice presence requires independent neutron/radar/optical spectroscopy.
3. **Thermal & Electrical Transient Physics:** Power sizing uses an idealized tracked PV array and constant base load; regolith thermal cooling curves, battery low-temperature derating, and eclipses/penumbrae are outside the planner battery budget.
4. **Lander-Scale Touchdown Safety:** Passing the `<= 5°` 20 m coarse slope threshold does not certify safe touchdown; high-resolution stereo/LROC NAC imagery (`~0.5–1.5 m/px`) is mandatory before landing selection.
